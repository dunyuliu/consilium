Invoke `shu-han`, fresh, on the idea and inputs passed as argument; with no
solicitation she lists the programs that fit and the PIs pick. Her workflow order is the contract: a requested output list adds files and never
drops a step, and nothing is drafted before `criteria_checklist.md` and
`literature_review.md` exist. For unpublished work her DOI/ID-only lookups hold;
topic searches with generic terms stay allowed. Her report names any skipped step.
